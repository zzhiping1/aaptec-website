0-Basic Course

零基础课程

⏱ Duration: 2 Weeks 学制：2 周

This foundational course is designed for individuals with no prior EV knowledge. You will learn the core principles of electric vehicles, essential safety protocols, basic diagnostic procedures, battery fundamentals, and motor basics. By the end of the course, you will be able to safely perform basic EV inspections and identify common issues.

本基础课程专为无新能源汽车经验的学员设计。课程涵盖电动汽车核心原理、基本安全规范、基础诊断流程、电池基础知识和电机基础。课程结束后，学员将能够独立进行基础新能源汽车检测并识别常见故障。

Key Topics
课程要点
  • EV fundamentals and architecture overview
  • 新能源汽车基本原理与架构概述
  • High-voltage safety protocols and PPE
  • 高压安全规范与个人防护
  • Basic diagnostic tool usage and interpretation
  • 基础诊断工具使用与结果解读
  • Battery pack structure and cell basics
  • 电池包结构与电芯基础
  • Electric motor types and operating principles
  • 电机类型与工作原理
  • Hands-on lab: basic vehicle inspection
  • 实操训练：基础车辆检测

Advanced Course

进阶学习

⏱ Duration: 4 Weeks 学制：4 周

Building on the fundamentals, this course dives deep into battery management systems, motor control strategies, advanced diagnostic techniques, high-voltage system safety, and OBD programming. Ideal for technicians seeking to work independently on modern EV powertrains and electronic systems.

在基础知识之上，本课程深入学习电池管理系统、电机控制策略、高级诊断技术、高压系统安全及OBD编程。适合希望独立维修新能源汽车动力总成和电子系统的技师。

Key Topics
课程要点
  • Battery Management System (BMS) architecture
  • 电池管理系统（BMS）架构
  • Motor control unit (MCU) diagnostics
  • 电机控制单元（MCU）诊断
  • Advanced OBD-II / UDS diagnostics and programming
  • 高级OBD-II / UDS诊断与编程
  • High-voltage interlock and isolation testing
  • 高压互锁与绝缘检测
  • Thermal management system troubleshooting
  • 热管理系统故障排查
  • Hands-on lab: full powertrain diagnosis
  • 实操训练：完整动力总成诊断

Master Course

大师课

⏱ Duration: 8 Weeks 学制：8 周

The Master Course is the pinnacle of AAPTEC training. Covering BMS programming at the firmware level, component-level board repair, reverse engineering of EV control systems, comprehensive ECU diagnostics, and complete three-electric system mastery. Graduates emerge as elite EV technicians capable of the most complex repair work.

大师课是AAPTEC培训体系的最高阶段。涵盖固件级BMS编程、元件级板级维修、新能源汽车控制系统逆向工程、全面ECU诊断及三电系统完整精通。毕业生将成为能够处理最复杂维修工作的精英新能源技师。

Key Topics

  • BMS固件编程与标定
  • Component-level PCB repair and micro-soldering
  • 元件级PCB维修与微焊接
  • Reverse engineering of EV control modules
  • 新能源汽车控制模块逆向工程
  • Comprehensive ECU cloning and adaptation
  • 全面ECU克隆与匹配
  • Complete three-electric system integration
  • 三电系统完整集成
  • Master lab: real-world complex repair scenarios
  • 大师实操：真实复杂维修场景
