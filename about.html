About AAPTEC

关于我们

As the largest new energy vehicle technical training organization in South-Central China, AAPtec provides integrated services including advanced EV three-electric maintenance training, professional talent dispatch and comprehensive transformation solutions for auto service startups.

AAPtec 为中国中南地区规模最大的新能源汽车技术培训机构，主营高阶三电维修实训、专业技师定向输送、汽服门店创业转型全案服务。

All instructors are veteran R&D engineers from top automotive OEMs with in-depth experience in new energy vehicle core component development, mastering official factory technical specifications. Supported by numerous verified successful projects, we have served more than 100 global clients.

教研师资全系头部主机厂原厂研发工程师，全员深耕新能源整车、三电系统正向研发，掌握原厂核心技术标准。依托大量落地标杆成功案例，业务辐射全球，累计服务海内外 100 余家合作客户。

Drawing on original-equipment R&D expertise, we deliver standardized high-level curricula and complete implementable operation packages, empowering technicians and service shops to embrace electrification, and standing as a benchmark for EV talent cultivation in the region.

我们依托原厂研发级技术底蕴，输出标准化高阶课程与整套落地运营方案，赋能技师精进技术、门店实现电动化升级，构筑区域新能源后市场人才培育标杆。
